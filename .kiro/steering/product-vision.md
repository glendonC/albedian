# Albedian Product Vision & Experience Steering

- **Product:** Albedian — *See (Beyond) the Night Sky.*
- **Summary:** *See the night. Understand the sky. Explore beyond it.*
- **Core Anchor:** The user's recorded night photograph or video (`SourceAsset`) is immutable and remains the persistent emotional and geometric anchor across all representations (`source`, `comparison`, `sky_registered`, `space`).
- **Observational Instrument Aesthetic:** Default to a minimal, dark (`#06090D`) observational interface where the recording occupies ~75–80% of the viewport. Avoid permanent node graphs, sci-fi HUD clutter, neon purple gradients, or decorative agent cursors.
- **Reversible Continuity:** Every departure into calibrated sky or 3D catalog space must checkpoint the exact media frame, timestamp, crop, selected finding, and audio state so `Back to Moment` restores the lived memory without loss.
