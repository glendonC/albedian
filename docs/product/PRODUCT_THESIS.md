# Albedian Product Thesis & Experience States

**Product:** Albedian  
**Tagline:** See (Beyond) the Night Sky.  
**Summary:** See the night. Understand the sky. Explore beyond it.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

Albedian is an observation-centered, scientifically grounded spatial computing and media restoration environment. It begins with a personal night-sky recording (photograph or video with original audio) and maintains that recording as the persistent anchor across four representations:

1. **Recorded (`source`):** Immutable original pixels, frame timestamps, crop, and original audio track.
2. **Restored (`comparison`):** Content-addressed media variants generated by an inspectable `ProcessingGraph` DAG (exposure/tone transfer, spatial/temporal denoise, point-source & foreground protection) synchronized to the authoritative source timeline.
3. **Calibrated Sky (`sky_registered`):** Angular celestial coordinates mapped directly onto source pixels via an independently verified World Coordinate System (`ObservationSolution`), or a clearly separated `Predicted Sky` when pixel calibration is unresolved.
4. **Scientific Space (`space`):** Catalog-backed 3D stellar geometry (`Gaia DR3` / Hipparcos subset with Bailer-Jones distance intervals), viewpoint rotation, sightline cone queries (`What's behind it?`), and epoch propagation (`Modeled Sky · 1026 CE`), with a single-action `Back to Moment` restoration of the exact source checkpoint.

## 2. The Four + One Orthogonal State Machines

Albedian never models the application as a single flat mode enum. A restoration job may run while the user inspects a calibrated sky.

| Machine | Legal States | Governing Invariant |
|---|---|---|
| **`Media`** | `empty`, `importing`, `ready`, `decode_error` | `ready` guarantees an immutable `SourceAsset` with verified SHA-256, dimensions, duration, and audio stream descriptors. |
| **`Restoration`** | `idle`, `analyzing`, `planning`, `queued`, `processing`, `evaluating`, `ready`, `failed`, `canceled` | Every attempt references an immutable `ProcessingGraph` revision and content address; progress derives strictly from real job events. |
| **`Calibration`** | `not_requested`, `detecting`, `solving`, `verifying`, `calibrated`, `predicted_only`, `ambiguous`, `unresolved` | Only `calibrated` (passing independent held-out inlier verification) authorizes pixel-registered catalog overlays. |
| **`Navigation`** | `source`, `comparison`, `sky_registered`, `transitioning`, `space`, `inspecting`, `returning` | Every transition out of `source` or across spatial viewpoints records a reversible `CameraCheckpoint` in `NavigationHistory`. |
| **`Voice`** | `off`, `permission`, `listening`, `hearing`, `interpreting`, `awaiting_tool`, `speaking`, `interrupted`, `unavailable` | Operates on structured session state through revision-checked `AlbedianAction` dispatch; never fabricates scientific or media state. |
