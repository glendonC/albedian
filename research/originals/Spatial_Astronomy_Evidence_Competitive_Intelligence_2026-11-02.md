## Executive Summary

The evidence supports a real observational and spatial-understanding problem, and a credible opportunity to connect personal sky capture with calibrated 3D exploration—but not a defensible claim that the entire category is unexplored. Several proposed competitor criticisms are contradicted by published capabilities:
- [Night Sky](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/night-sky/id475772902) already offers planetary-surface portals.
- [NASA Eyes on Exoplanets](https://science.nasa.gov/tutorials/eyes-on-exoplanets-tutorial/) connects a location-specific Earth view to exoplanet systems.
- [WorldWide Telescope](https://docs.worldwidetelescope.org/tour-authoring-guide/1/video-abstracts/) supports importing astronomical imagery.

## 1. Scientific and Empirical Evidence

### 1.1 Light Pollution Removes Access
- **Falchi et al. (2016), *Science Advances*:** ["The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4928945/)
  - **>80% of humanity** (~83% globally, and >99% of US and European populations) lives under light-polluted skies.
  - **>1/3 of humanity** (including 60% of Europeans and nearly 80% of North Americans) cannot see the Milky Way.
- **Kyba et al. (2023), *Science* (DOI `10.1126/science.abq7781`):** ["Citizen scientists report global rapid reductions in the visibility of stars from 2011 to 2022"](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36656932/) · [SciSpace entry](https://scispace.com/papers/citizen-scientists-report-global-rapid-reductions-in-the-3dkstsy8) · [ScienceNews coverage](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/light-pollution-night-sky-bright-citizen-science)
  - Analyzed 51,351 citizen-science observations (2011–2022); observed changes were consistent with sky-brightness growth of **~7–10% annually (central average: 9.6% per year)**.

## 4. Product Positioning & Minimum Convincing Demonstration Summary

- **Consumer Headline:** *"Turn your night-sky photo into a calibrated 3D journey. Identify the lights you captured, then travel to the worlds behind them."*
- **Scientific Qualifier:** *"Your image anchors the view. Astronomical catalogs place the objects. Measured data and clearly labeled simulations build the journey."*
- **Developer / SDK Headline:** *"A capture-to-cosmos SDK: connect sky imagery to identified celestial objects and navigable scientific scenes."*
- **The Proof Moment:** *"The category earns its distinction when personal observational evidence remains legible throughout the spectacle—not when the spectacle merely begins with a photo upload."*
